# Minion Run progression, powers, and presentation
Objective: Turn Minion Run into a 20-banana escape game with staged pursuers, player gadgets, and a polished ending.
Branch: `master` (direct-main delivery explicitly requested). Delivery: `exception-ok`, nine ordered commits pushed immediately to `origin/master`. Runner: `node --test tests/minion-run-rules.test.cjs` plus local Playwright browser smoke checks. Forecast: approximately 900 authored changed lines.

## Specs

1. **S1 — Twenty-banana objective and victory.** Implement the user's requested goal, quoted verbatim: "que la meta sea 20 bananas agarrar y ganas". Every collected banana advances the objective by exactly one, including golden bananas. Reaching 20 ends active gameplay in victory; score cannot skip the win because of a special banana.
2. **S2 — Banana milestone progression.** Implement one-shot milestones at 5, 10, and 15 bananas. Preserve the requested sequence: "cuando agarre las primeros 5 bananas Gru se vuelve más rápido", "cuando llega las 10 bananas Gru comienza a invocar minions morados que me persiguen", and "cuando llega las 15 bananas Esto Gru se transforme ... El macho". Thresholds use `>=` with one-shot flags so no milestone can be skipped.
3. **S3 — Purple pursuers and El Macho form.** At 10 bananas, spawn two purple minions that chase the player, steer less effectively than Gru, and slow/push the player instead of directly removing a life. At 15 bananas, replace Gru's visible form with an El Macho-inspired stronger form and begin the final chase phase.
4. **S4 — Progress HUD.** Show `current / 20` bananas and visible milestone markers for 5, 10, 15, and 20. The active and completed phases must be visually distinguishable on desktop and touch layouts.
5. **S5 — Golden banana power pickup.** Golden bananas still advance the objective by one and grant one random player gadget charge. The player holds one gadget at a time; a new golden banana replaces the held gadget. This implements the accepted recommendation that the player uses Gru's gadgets to escape.
6. **S6 — Freeze ray.** Implement "El de congelar" as a forward-facing player gadget that freezes Gru and purple minions temporarily, with clear HUD feedback and a blue visual/audio effect.
7. **S7 — Fart gun.** Implement "El de tirar pedos" as a close-range cloud that pushes nearby pursuers away and temporarily slows/confuses them, with a distinct green visual/audio effect.
8. **S8 — Tentacle gun.** Implement "El de los tentáculos" as a forward-facing restraint that roots the closest valid pursuer temporarily, with a distinct purple visual/audio effect.
9. **S9 — Keyboard and touch gadget controls.** Add one keyboard action and one visible touch button for using the equipped gadget. Existing movement, sprint, jump, pause, look, and mute controls must keep their previous behavior.
10. **S10 — Milestone presentation.** Add short non-blocking or briefly slowed milestone cinematics, messages, and sounds for the 5, 10, and 15 banana transitions. Player control must resume automatically and the same milestone must not replay.
11. **S11 — Visual effects and transformation animation.** Add readable visual effects for gadget use, purple minion arrival, and the El Macho transformation. The transformation may be an authored scale/color/model transition rather than a skeletal animation.
12. **S12 — Victory presentation and time score.** Add a dedicated victory screen showing collected bananas, completion time, remaining lives, and a deterministic run score derived from those values. Keep the existing caught/game-over flow available when lives reach zero.
13. **S13 — Paired commit cadence.** Follow the user's exact delivery constraint: "cada función ... lo agrupes en dos", crossing G01/G02/G03 boundaries when necessary; "si te queda solo uno al final, ya ese que queda solo le haces un commit y un push"; and "ni bien terminas haces el commit y push". Each task below is one commit, verified before commit and pushed immediately to `origin/master` using the authorized GitHub CLI credential session.
